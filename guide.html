How this course works

About three minutes to read. You watch a short video, read a little, then try it straight away. Nothing here is a trick, and you can repeat anything.

1. The shape of a chapter

  1. Overview: what you will be able to do by the end.
  2. Lessons (six per chapter): each lesson has three parts.
    1. Watch: a 2 to 3 minute video. Sam, your virtual tutor, explains the idea while a live 3D animation shows it step by step, with captions and soft background music.
    2. Read: short lesson notes with real examples. Press Read the notes aloud if you prefer to listen.
    3. Try it: 2 to 4 activities. Each one opens after you have tried the one before.
  3. Chapter quiz: 8 to 10 questions, one try each, with an explanation after every answer.
  4. Boss level: a longer made-up case where you make a series of decisions as the leader in the room.
  5. Glossary: plain-English meanings of the key terms. Open it whenever you like.

Use the row of buttons at the top of each chapter to jump anywhere. A tick means that part is done.

4. Points, levels and stars

  • Every activity is worth up to 10 XP (experience points). The chapter quiz and the boss level are worth up to 30 XP each.
  • You can try again on any activity. Your best score is kept. A retry counts at half value, so it pays to think on the first go. The chapter quiz is the exception: you can retake it and the full score counts.
  • Stars: 3 stars for 90% or more, 2 for 70% or more, 1 for 40% or more.
  • Levels: Newcomer, Explorer, Navigator, Strategist and Champion. You move up as your XP grows.
  • A chapter is complete once you have attempted every activity, the quiz and the boss level.

5. Your certificate

When all four chapters are complete, the home page lets you type your name and print or save a certificate of completion. Choose "Save as PDF" in the print window if you want a file.

6. Good to know

  • Your progress is saved in this browser only. If you switch device, use a private window, or clear your browser data, it starts again.
  • The course works on phones, tablets and computers, and follows your device's light or dark setting. If your device cannot show 3D, the video still plays with Sam and captions.
  • If you have turned on “reduce motion” on your device, the 3D camera stays still.
  • Explanations appear after each answer. Read them, especially when you were wrong. That is where most of the learning happens.
  • Case studies and company names are used as lessons, not accusations. Any hospital or trust in the scenarios is made up.
  • Rules, laws and figures change. Treat the course as a learning aid and check current guidance before acting on it.
